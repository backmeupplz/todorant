# Ad variant: ADHD-friendly planner

A custom product page for the Apple Search Ads test (prime-radiant experiments/2026-10-28-todorant-search-ads.md).
appstore/cpp.py renders its screenshots and sends it to App Review. It makes no medical claims: it describes how the
app is built (one task on screen, a date for every todo), which is what people searching "adhd planner" look for.

## Name
Ads: ADHD-friendly planner

## Promotional text (max 170)
Long lists make it hard to start. Todorant shows one task at a time and gives every todo a date, so there is always one clear next step. Free, with no ads.
